## Why

[`README.md`](../../README.md) is one line: a bare `<img>` tag pointing at a
`github.com/user-attachments/…` URL. No title, no description, no live link, no
setup instructions, no tech stack. An earlier commit deleted `client/README.md`
(the 54-line Vite default) and nothing replaced it.

For a portfolio project this is the highest-value fix in the whole audit — and
also the one I can least finish alone, because it needs your live URL,
screenshots, and licence choice.

## Why a draft, not a rewrite

Overwriting `README.md` with placeholder URLs is worse than leaving it alone: a
portfolio repo with `https://your-deployed-url.app` in it reads as abandoned. So
this plan writes `docs/README.draft.md` and **does not touch the live file**.
You review, fill three blanks, and move it.
