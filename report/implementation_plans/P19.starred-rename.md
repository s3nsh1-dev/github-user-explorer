## Why

Three spellings of one concept across nine files:

| Current | Problem |
|---|---|
| `staredList`, `staredProfiles`, `checkStared` | "stared" (looking at) vs "starred" (bookmarked) |
| `useStaredUserList` (file) exporting `useStartedUserList` | a third spelling — "started" |
| `StaredRepositories` | renders starred **users**, not repositories |
| `GitHubUser.hirable` | API field is `hireable` |
| `"Response for LongType was not ok"` | LongType → LoginType |

A reviewer reading three spellings of one concept draws conclusions. Cheap to
fix while the surface is small.

**This is a pure refactor — zero behaviour change.** If the diff shows anything
other than renames, it is wrong.
